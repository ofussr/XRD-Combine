XRD Combine — Scientific Data and Third-Party Notices
=====================================================

This file records scientific datasets and principal third-party components
used by XRD Combine.  Exact package versions are determined by the build
environment.  When making a binary distribution, retain this file beside the
executable and retain any additional notices supplied by the exact wheels and
runtime used for that build.


SCIENTIFIC DATA
---------------

Waasmaier–Kirfel atomic scattering-factor coefficients
File: xrd_workbench/resources/f0_WaasKirf.dat
Source collection: DABAX (DAtaBAse for X-ray applications)
Source: https://github.com/oasys-kit/DabaxFiles

Scientific reference:
D. Waasmaier and A. Kirfel, "New Analytical Scattering-Factor Functions for
Free Atoms and Ions", Acta Crystallographica Section A 51 (1995), 416–431.
https://doi.org/10.1107/S0108767394013292

DABAX repository notice:
Copyright (c) 2022 Manuel Sanchez Del Rio
Licensed under the MIT License reproduced below.


Atomic drawing colours and covalent radii
File: xrd_workbench/resources/atom_styles.json
Derived from mendeleev 1.2.0 data.
Source: https://github.com/lmmentel/mendeleev
Copyright (c) 2015 Lukasz Mentel
Licensed under the MIT License reproduced below.

The runtime file contains only atomic numbers, element symbols, Jmol, CPK and
MOLCAS GV colour fields, and Pyykko/Cordero covalent-radius fields.  XRD
Combine does not include or claim a VESTA colour scheme.


Space-group settings and symmetry operations
File: xrd_workbench/resources/space_groups.json
Derived from the spglib 2.7.0 symmetry database.
Source: https://github.com/spglib/spglib
Copyright (c) 2024, Spglib team
Licensed under the BSD 3-Clause License reproduced below.
